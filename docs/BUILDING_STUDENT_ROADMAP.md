# Building student learning roadmap

BuildWise's initial university audience is students in B.Sc./B.Tech Building programmes. The [NUC Environmental Sciences CCMAS](https://www.nuc.edu.ng/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/Environmental-Sciences-CCMAS-2023-FINAL.pdf) covers construction, materials, graphics, measurement, building services, maintenance, management, integrated studio, SIWES, project reports, and BIM. Individual universities can vary their courses and sequence. [OAU's published outline](https://building.oauife.edu.ng/files/course_outline.pdf) and [FUTA's course synopses](https://bdg.futa.edu.ng/home/courses/200) are starting references, not verified current course maps for BuildWise.

## Available in the student MVP

- Telegram Study & Solve handles text, question photos, calculations, theory, and PDFs.
- Quick Practice supports multiple-choice sessions; see [verification steps](QUICK_PRACTICE_VERIFICATION.md).
- Sketch and building-photo analysis provide preliminary learning assistance, with safety boundaries.
- Students can use history, saved answers, goals, reminders, and progress tracking.
- Conceptual sketch-view generation exists. Its outputs are not construction-ready drawings or professional approvals.

BuildWise Pro is a separate, disabled professional beta. It is not a student Premium plan. No student payment or Premium entitlement system is available.

## Product boundaries

- Course-specific content needs a current local syllabus and permission for any third-party source material.
- Digital lessons support supervised workshop and site experience; they do not replace it.
- AI analyses and generated views cannot certify structural safety, legal compliance, permit approval, or professional sign-off.
- Do not claim a university, department, or professional body has approved BuildWise without recorded authorization.
